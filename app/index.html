How we check a deal

  1. Read the price. The sale price and the usual price are read at the store itself, or in a deals source that names the store. The source is printed on every deal.
  2. Check the “was”. A discount only counts against the price the store actually charged — not a list price nobody paid. Where a source flagged an inflated list price, we say so.
  3. Ask if it is really a deal. NEW LOW means a source confirms the lowest price ever recorded. STRONG DEAL is 40% off or more. SOLID DEAL is 20–39% off. ROUTINE SALE is under 20% — listed honestly, because a small real cut beats a big fake one.
  4. Stamp the time. Every deal shows when its price was checked. Prices move during Black Friday week — always check the live listing before you buy.
